# Draft theme lists, per industry

Status: first draft for Tom to edit. Not yet used by anything.

These are the lists that step 1 of `docs/toms-template-themes-plan.md` asks for: the themes a classifier would assign to each post. They were drafted by reading a sample of real captions from each industry (30 to 46 per industry, spread across brands and platforms) together with the most frequent hashtags and words. They have not been tested by classifying posts, so expect to merge, split and rename.

A theme says what a post is about. It does not say how it is told (that is the hook) or what the author thinks of the brand.

## Used in every industry

**Hooks** (how the post is told)

- Tutorial or how-to
- Review or first impressions
- Routine or day in the life
- Haul or unboxing
- Comparison
- Deal or promotion
- News or announcement
- Event coverage
- Humour or entertainment
- Opinion or commentary
- Listing or sale advert
- Giveaway or competition
- Other

**About the brand** (the sample shows this matters: many tracked posts are not about the brand)

- About the brand
- Mentions the brand in passing
- A different meaning of the name (a web tool called Vite filed under Veet; astrology filed under Venus)
- Unrelated (a baseball recap filed under an NFL team; a local news story filed under a destination)

**Two themes every list ends with**

- Other: about the brand, but fits no theme
- Unknown: no caption, or a caption that says nothing

## What the sample showed

- **Off-topic posts are common.** In every industry the sample contained posts that are not about the brand. That makes the "about the brand" field as important as the theme.
- **Some lists contain themes that are not marketing conversations.** Used-car listings, local news, bar specials and reseller adverts are large in the data. They are kept as themes so they can be seen and, if wanted, filtered out.
- **Captions on long videos say little.** Many YouTube descriptions are channel boilerplate. Those posts will land in Unknown without a title or a transcript.
